Version 3.0.0 - The app has been rebuilt from the ground up. New: a reminder before a lecture starts (15, 30 or 60 minutes ahead) and a native iOS app with its own timetable widget. Change alerts now recognise a moved lecture as a move, never report lectures already over, and look four weeks ahead. The background check runs hourly instead of every 15 minutes. Switching tabs no longer reloads. Credentials are cleared on reinstall. Fixes a crash when opening the settings.
